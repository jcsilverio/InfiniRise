Privacy

This page explains what information infinirisestudios.com collects when you visit, and how it is used.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count visits. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal information. It records aggregate data such as page views, referring sites, browser type, and country.

Advertising measurement

This site loads the Google Ads tag, which uses cookies and similar technologies to measure whether visits and actions on this site came from our Google ads. Google may collect device and browser identifiers and your IP address for this purpose. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

Hosting

Our hosting provider may log basic request information, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@infinirisestudios.com. InfiniRise Studios LLC, Georgia, United States.
